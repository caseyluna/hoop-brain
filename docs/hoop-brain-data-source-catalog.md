# hoop-brain — Data Source Catalog

**Companion to the Backend PRD (§7).** The full menu of what exists, how to get it, and what role each source should play — so you can pick and choose deliberately. Access methods verified July 2026; these change (Patreon tiers, APIs, ToS), so re-verify before wiring anything into the pipeline.

**Role legend:**

- **Pipeline** — automatable, wire into ingestion-engine.
- **Curated import** — pull/scrape into the maintained internal dataset with manual override.
- **Reference** — human-readable cross-check or rules source; not ingested.
- **Methodology** — published formulas/approaches to _rebuild yourself_ rather than pull.

## Recommendations (opinionated, per your stated approach)

1. **Wire in now (free, automatable):** `nba_api`, `wehoop`, **DARKO CSV** (trivial win — nightly, free, NBA-ID-keyed), Her Hoop Stats cap data (WNBA contract seed), Spotrac+RealGM scrape (NBA contract seed).
2. **Evaluate before Stage 4:** pbpstats API vs. building your own possession parser — potentially weeks saved on the project's hardest data-engineering stage.
3. **Rebuild, don't pull:** everything Crafted NBA computes (ToS + it's exactly the priority-4 muscle you want to build); BPM/VORP (formulas are public); your own RAPM once lineups exist.
4. **Paid metrics — decided: rebuild, don't subscribe.** EPM-style and LEBRON-style metrics get recreated in model-engine from their published methodologies, validated against public leaderboards and DARKO's free CSV — the rebuild _is_ the learning. The one subscription still worth weighing on its own merits: Dunc'd On Prime, as cap education + the gold-standard human cross-check while building the cap engine — arguably the most goal-aligned $100 in this catalog.
5. **Respect the small-site reality:** DARKO, databallr, Crafted, HHS are labors of love. Honor ToS (Crafted: no scraping), cite where asked (HHS), rate-limit everything, and prefer published download paths over scraping wherever both exist.
